# Unit 9: Summary

Supervision connects human decisions with evidence throughout a task.

## What you learned

In this module, you learned how to:

- **Assign human control points and decision owners.**

- **Use requirements and evidence to approve, reject, request changes, or clarify.**

- **Redirect a running session within its approved scope and limits.**

- **Find a failure's cause and choose a permitted correction or escalation.**

- **Stop a session, check remaining activity, and keep recovery evidence.**

- **Accept a result only when evidence supports its requirements and agreed limits.**
