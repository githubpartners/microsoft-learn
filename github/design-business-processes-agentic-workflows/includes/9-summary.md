## **Unit 9 — Summary**

In this module, you learned to design an agentic workflow before implementing it. A suitable task has a useful purpose, available information, measurable criteria, and acceptable risk.

You can now:

- Assess repetition, ambiguity, stability, volume, value, and accountability.

- Map inputs, transformations, decisions, exceptions, tools, and outputs.

- Set agent-owned, human-approved, and human-only decision boundaries.

- Choose patterns for triage, reporting, monitoring, review, classification, and escalation.

- Specify the workflow's behavior, access, validation, failure handling, and completion.

- Review and version changes, preserve a known-good baseline, and plan recovery.

The key distinction is between preparing work and authorizing its consequences. Make that boundary visible in the process, specification, and implementation.
